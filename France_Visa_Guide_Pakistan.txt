FRANCE — Visa Application Guide for Pakistani Applicants
Source type: Independent informational summary based on the official France-Visas portal, Campus France Pakistan, and the AEG Travel Services one-pager (France's outsourced visa partner in Pakistan).
Covers: Student (Long-Stay) Visa, including the mandatory Études en France (EEF) procedure for Pakistani applicants, and the Short-Stay (Schengen) Visa for tourism, family visits and short study/training.

MAJOR CHANGE FROM 1 AUGUST 2026
The minimum financial resources required for a long-stay French student visa/residence permit has risen from €615/month to €877.50 per month (about €10,530/year), replacing a threshold unchanged since 2002. This applies to applications submitted from 1 August 2026 onward.

1. KNOW YOUR VISA TYPE
- Short-Stay (Schengen) Visa: Tourism, business, family visits, short training/conferences, study ≤90 days. Up to 90 days in any 180-day period, across the Schengen Area.
- Long-Stay Visa — VLS-TS: Study, work, or long-stay for personal/family reasons exceeding 90 days; acts as a residence permit once validated. 3 months to 1 year (renewable via a prefecture for longer stays).
- Long-Stay Visa — "residence card to be applied for": Same purposes, but requires an in-person prefecture visit within 2 months of arrival to get a residence permit.
- Student examination visa (étudiant-concours): Sitting an entrance exam/interview for a French higher-education institution. Max. 90 days; can be regularised locally if you pass.

2. SHORT-STAY (SCHENGEN) VISA
The short-stay visa covers tourist travel, business trips, family visits, short training programmes, internships, conferences, corporate meetings, or paid activities of any kind, provided total stay does not exceed 90 days.
2.1 Uniform Schengen short-stay visa: valid for transit through or stay in part or all of the Schengen Area; cannot exceed 90 days in any 180 consecutive days.
2.2 Which mission to apply to: France as sole destination; France as main destination by duration/purpose; or France as first point of entry with no clear main destination.
2.3 Multiple-entry "circulation visa": frequent travellers may be eligible for a multiple-entry visa valid from 6 months up to 5 years, subject to proof of genuine need and reliability track record; eligibility and validity period determined case-by-case.

3. LONG-STAY VISA — GENERAL CATEGORIES
Any stay exceeding 90 days requires a long-stay visa in advance regardless of nationality. Valid for 3 months to 1 year; to stay longer, apply for a residence permit at a French prefecture before expiry. During validity, functions like a Schengen visa for travel elsewhere in the Schengen Area (up to 90 days in any 180-day period).
3.1 Two main categories:
- VLS-TS: for stays of 1 year or less; exempts from separate residence permit application, but must validate within 3 months of arrival via the ANEF portal.
- Long-stay visa with "residence card to be applied for": must present at prefecture within 2 months of arrival (in some cases, e.g. "Passeport-Talent" scheme, a multi-annual permit may be issued).
A temporary long-stay visa (VLS-T) also exists for those certain not to extend beyond visa validity.

4. STUDENT / LONG-STAY VISA — FULL DETAIL
4.1 Eligibility: 18+ years old; already chosen training course/programme; accepted by a recognised higher-education establishment; depending on age/enrolment year, may need to follow Parcoursup procedure and schedule.
4.2 Pakistan is an EEF-procedure country: virtually all Pakistani residents applying to study in France (Bachelor's to most Master's and PhD) must go through the Études en France (EEF) platform before applying for a visa. Main exception: PhD candidates in Pakistan applying under a Doctoral Fellowship.
4.3 Which visa: training/course ≤3 months → short-stay visa; training/course >3 months → long-stay visa (VLS-TS), requiring arrival formalities.
4.4 Financial resources requirement (updated 1 August 2026):
- Previous threshold: €615/month (unchanged since 2002).
- New threshold (from 1 Aug 2026): €877.50/month (≈€10,530/year), set at 47% of the gross SMIC.
- From Decree No. 2026-526 (22 June 2026); applies to any application submitted on/after 1 August 2026 — submission date matters, not renewal expiry date. Threshold is indexed and may be revised at least once a year.
- Acceptable evidence: personal savings/bank statements (own or sponsor's), blocked account or guaranteed funds, scholarship award letter, official sponsor/family letter covering full stay duration.
4.5 Visa fees for students: EEF-procedure countries (incl. Pakistan): €50 (indicative). Non-EEF countries: €99 (indicative).
4.6 Supporting documents: exact list generated by France-Visas visa wizard; at minimum, certificate of enrolment from the accepting institution plus standard identity, financial and accommodation documents.
4.7 Special student situations:
- Entrance-exam applicants (étudiant-concours): short-stay visa up to 90 days, regularisable at prefecture if passed (not available to Algerian nationals under Franco-Algerian Agreement).
- Medical students (DFMS/DFMSA): long-stay visa equivalent to residence permit for studies, selected via Embassy's cultural services during Q1.
- Student mobility (EU programme/inter-institution agreement): visa marked "Étudiant – mobilité" — VLS-TS (≤12 months) or long-stay visa with prefecture registration (>12 months).
4.8 Life as a student: Tuition varies from a few hundred euros to over €10,000/year at private institutions. Accommodation: contact host institution's international relations office; provide temporary address for first 3 months. Working while studying: non-Algerian foreign students up to 964 hours (60% of full-time hours); Algerian nationals limited to 50%; part-time work is secondary income only. Health cover: free/mandatory French social security registration for non-European students under 28; EEA students with EHIC or private insurance covering all medical risks are exempt; Social Security reimburses ~60% of costs.

5. THE ÉTUDES EN FRANCE (EEF) PROCEDURE FOR PAKISTAN
Run locally by Campus France Pakistan (public agency under the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs); manages enrolment end-to-end up to visa application.
5.1 Who must use EEF: all Pakistan residents applying to study in France (any level) except PhD applicants under a Doctoral Fellowship. Applicants living outside Pakistan must use the EEF platform of their country of residence.
5.2 Step-by-step EEF process:
Step 1. Create EEF account (registered email is the only communication channel — never share it).
Step 2. Complete personal information and upload required supporting documents.
Step 3. Select up to 7 Master's programmes or 3 Bachelor's programmes; submit with payment by annual deadline (e.g., file submission by 30 November, payment by 12 December in a recent cycle).
Step 4. After file validation, Campus France Pakistan invites you to an academic interview booked only via EEF portal, held at the nearest Alliance Française (Islamabad, Lahore, or Karachi). Bring original documents plus EEF fee payment receipt.
Step 5. Institutions review and indicate admission decisions on EEF (recent cycle: results by 30 April, ~1 month to confirm final choice).
Step 6. Once final programme confirmed and payment validated, receive the Études en France Certificate — required before booking your visa appointment.
No walk-ins; strict deadlines — missing any EEF deadline results in an abandoned file requiring reapplication in a later cycle.
5.3 EEF fee payment: paid to Alliance Française Karachi by bank transfer/deposit, reference format EEF/SURNAME/EEF NUMBER. Must declare payment on EEF profile and email scanned deposit slip to the Embassy's cooperation office within one week of the payment request.

6. THE AEG APPOINTMENT PROCESS IN PAKISTAN
All visa appointments (ordinary passports, short-stay and long-stay/student) are booked through AEG Travel Services (Pvt) Ltd, France's official outsourced visa partner (offices in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Multan, Sialkot).
AEG does not authorise travel agents/third parties to book appointments. Self-appearance is mandatory (or a blood relative may book on your behalf). Any party guaranteeing an appointment or outcome is making a false claim.
6.1 Steps to book a student visa appointment:
Step 1. Complete the France-Visas application form fully online.
Step 2. Deposit PKR 30,000 by bank transfer to Alliance Française Karachi's Askari Bank account (reference must include full name and EEF application number, e.g. PKXX-XXXXXX) — prerequisite before AEG allows booking.
Step 3. Obtain the Études en France Certificate and Campus France payment receipt — appointments will not be issued without both.
Step 4. Book appointment with AEG. Appointments are prioritised by course start date on the EEF certificate; if slots are filled, email AEG directly.
Step 5. Attend appointment at the French Embassy (Diplomatic Enclave, Islamabad) with CV, motivation letter, admission/invitation confirmation letter, scholarship confirmation (if applicable), all previous degrees and transcripts, other supporting education documents, plus the full France-Visas checklist.
6.2 On interview day: Islamabad applicants report to the Diplomatic Shuttle Service (3rd Avenue, near Quaid-e-Azam University Road, G-5, Islamabad) at least 30 minutes early. Biometric data (photo + fingerprints of both hands) is collected; children under 12 exempt from fingerprinting. Interview dates are tentative and can change without notice; cannot be arranged by phone/email — only via official AEG booking.

7. FEES SUMMARY
- Student visa fee (EEF-procedure countries incl. Pakistan): €50 (indicative)
- Student visa fee (non-EEF countries): €99 (indicative)
- EEF platform fee: deposited to Alliance Française Karachi, amount specified individually per applicant/programme
- AEG appointment deposit (student applicants): PKR 30,000, prerequisite before appointment booking
- AEG service charge — Islamabad office: €8.12/file (paid in PKR cash)
- AEG service charge — Punjab: €11.10/file (PKR cash)
- AEG service charge — Sindh: €10.00/file (PKR cash)
- AEG service charge — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: €11.50/file (PKR cash)
- AEG service charge — Balochistan: €11.50/file (PKR cash)
- Schengen short-stay visa fee: per France-Visas fee schedule

8. PROCESSING TIMES
- EEF review (initial file check): ~1 week for initial status update.
- Visa processing (from full file submission): generally 2 weeks to 2 months depending on complexity/completeness.
- Étudiant-concours visa: finalised only once exam results are known, typically within ~90 days.
Given the sequential process (EEF review, academic interview, programme confirmation, EEF certificate, AEG deposit, appointment booking), start as early as possible, ideally as soon as the EEF campaign opens.

9. SUPPORTING-DOCUMENT FRAMEWORK
France-Visas uses an interactive visa wizard for the precise document list per case. General families:
- Identity: Passport (valid well beyond stay), CNIC, photographs — all applicants
- Application: France-Visas online form finalised/printed — all applicants
- Purpose of travel: Certificate of enrolment (students); invitation/hosting certificate (family visits); itinerary/hotel bookings (tourism) — category-specific
- Financial means: Bank statements, blocked account, scholarship letter, or sponsor's letter (students: ≥€877.50/month from 1 Aug 2026) — all applicants, thresholds vary
- Insurance: Travel/health insurance meeting Schengen minimums — short-stay applicants primarily
- Accommodation: Temporary or permanent address in France for the visa period — all applicants
- Academic: Études en France Certificate, admission letter, past degrees/transcripts — student applicants
Digitisation: France-Visas allows uploading digital copies before appointment (initially for long-stay student visa applicants in outsourced-process countries) — documents must be legible and correctly filed; errors may cause additional on-the-spot service charges.

10. POST-ARRIVAL REQUIREMENTS
- Validate VLS-TS within 3 months of arrival via the ANEF online portal (mandatory, with a validation fee).
- If visa carries "residence card to be applied for" mention: present at local prefecture within 2 months of arrival.
- Register for French social security (mandatory and free for non-EEA students under 28) via the dedicated student health-insurance registration website.
- Keep proof of accommodation address for first 3 months (may be requested by prefecture/ANEF).

11. APPLICATION CHECKLIST
Student applicants:
- Create EEF account, submit programme choices and documents by campaign deadline
- Pay EEF fee to Alliance Française Karachi, declare payment on EEF portal within 1 week
- Attend academic interview at Alliance Française with all original documents
- Confirm final programme choice once admitted; obtain Études en France Certificate
- Complete France-Visas application form online
- Deposit PKR 30,000 with Alliance Française Karachi to unlock AEG appointment booking
- Book AEG appointment, referencing EEF number and certificate
- Attend Embassy interview in Islamabad with the full supporting-document set
- After arrival: validate VLS-TS via ANEF within 3 months, register for social security if eligible
Short-stay (tourist/family/short study) applicants:
- Use France-Visas visa wizard to generate personalised document list
- Complete France-Visas online application form
- Gather identity, financial, insurance, accommodation and purpose-of-travel documents
- Book appointment through AEG Travel Services
- Attend appointment with the complete, correctly ordered document set
