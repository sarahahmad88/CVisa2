ITALY — Visa Application Guide for Pakistani Applicants
Source type: Independent informational summary based on requirements published by the Consulate General of Italy in Karachi and the Embassy of Italy in Islamabad (via their VFS Global one-pager information pages).
Covers: Short-Stay Schengen (tourist) visa and the National (Study) visa.

TWO APPLICATION CENTRES
Pakistani applicants apply through VFS Global-run Visa Application Centres tied to two Italian missions: the Consulate General of Italy in Karachi (applicants resident in Sindh/Balochistan) and the Embassy of Italy in Islamabad (applicants resident in Punjab, KP, Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan and AJK). Fees, checklists and processing guidance differ slightly between the two centres — always use the centre matching your residence.

1. KNOW YOUR VISA TYPE
- Schengen Short-Stay Visa ("C"): Tourism, visiting friends/family, business, medical, religious, sports, short conferences, or study/training under 90 days. Up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the whole Schengen Area.
- National/Long-Stay Visa ("D") — Study: Enrolment on a course of study lasting more than 90 days. Duration of study programme, followed by a residence permit (permesso di soggiorno) obtained after arrival.
Rule of thumb: course ≤90 days → short-stay Schengen study visa; course >90 days → National/Long-Stay study visa.

2. ISLAMABAD VS KARACHI — JURISDICTION
- Consulate General of Italy, Karachi: applicants resident in Sindh and Balochistan. Service fee: €30/application (cash, at VFS centre). Recommends applying 3–4 weeks before travel.
- Embassy of Italy, Islamabad: applicants resident in Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Service fee: €30/application (cash, local currency, at VFS centre). Requires applying at least 30 days before travel; applications with less than 30 days' notice are not accepted.
Both jurisdictions use VFS Global as the outsourced Visa Application Centre operator, with separate one-pager sites per city.

3. SHORT-STAY (SCHENGEN TOURIST) VISA
3.1 Visa & Service Fees:
- Schengen Visa — adult: €80 (approx. PKR 24,780)
- Schengen Visa — children 6–12 years: €40 (approx. PKR 12,390)
- Schengen Visa — children under 6 years: €0 (exempt)
A separate VFS service fee of €30 per application is also charged in cash at the Visa Application Centre in addition to the visa fee. PKR amounts set at consulate's exchange rate, revised periodically.
3.2 Who is exempt from the visa fee: children under 6; pupils, students and their accompanying teachers travelling for study/educational training; researchers from third countries conducting scientific research (per EU recommendation 2005/761/EC); representatives of non-profit organisations aged 25 or under attending seminars/conferences/sports/cultural/educational events; participants aged 25 or under in non-profit-organised events; spouses, children and dependent ascendants of an EU/EEA national exercising free-movement rights in Italy, when visiting that person.
3.3 Documents Required:
- Short-stay visa application form, fully completed (address, phone numbers, parents' names); for minors, signed by both parents.
- Two recent (max. 6 months old) colour passport-size photographs on white background, unedited.
- Original passport valid at least 3 months beyond the visa's expiry, with at least 2 blank pages.
- If refused a Schengen visa in the last 2 years: written explanation of trip purpose and the earlier refusal.
- Original and photocopy of NADRA Family Registration Certificate (English or certified translation).
- For minors under 18 travelling with only one parent: written consent from the other parent plus a copy of that parent's CNIC.
- Signed letter from employer (name, position, salary, employment duration, employer's address/contact, purpose & duration of trip) — or, if a student, a signed letter from school/college/university.
- Copy of return flight booking(s).
- Proof of accommodation for the entire trip (confirmed hotel booking or rental agreement with name, dates, address) or detailed itinerary for package tours.
- Travel/medical insurance of at least €30,000 covering the whole Schengen stay, including repatriation.
- Copy of CNIC.
- Proof of personal income: bank account maintenance certificate, bank statements for last 6 months, National Tax Number certificate for last 2 years with proof of tax paid, and last 3 salary slips.
Incomplete files are rejected outright; forged/fake documents are reported to authorities. Third-country nationals resident in Pakistan must additionally prove legality and duration of residence in Pakistan.
3.4 Processing Time: Karachi jurisdiction — apply at least 3–4 weeks before travel. Islamabad jurisdiction — apply at least 30 days in advance; less than 30 days' notice is not accepted.
3.5 Forms to Download: Schengen Visa Application Form; Personal Details Form (mandatory, Karachi jurisdiction); Host Declaration/Invitation Letter for Tourist Visa; list of approved insurance companies; Financial resources requirement sheet (Islamabad jurisdiction).

4. STUDENT / NATIONAL (LONG-STAY) VISA
National ("D") visa for study programmes lasting more than 90 days. This section reflects the Islamabad jurisdiction one-pager; Karachi jurisdiction applicants should confirm the equivalent checklist as some documents may be listed slightly differently.
4.1 Visa Fee: National (Long-Stay) Visa: €116 (approx. PKR 35,940). A VFS service fee of €30 (local currency, cash) also applies.
4.2 Documents Required:
- Visa application form, completed and signed.
- Two recent passport-size photographs.
- Passport valid at least 3 months after the visa's expiry.
- Admission/acceptance letter from the Italian university or institute.
- Proof of a study grant (if awarded) by the Italian government, a recognised foundation/cultural institute, an international organisation, a foreign government, or a university — or, if no grant or an insufficient one, proof of own or family's financial means.
- Proof of appropriate lodging arrangements in Italy, plus funds for the return journey (a return air ticket can serve as proof of the latter).
- Travel/health insurance valid across the Schengen states, covering the full stay, with a minimum liability of €30,000.
No fee for stays under 90 days — a study visa for 90 days or less uses the short-stay Schengen study visa instead of the National visa.
A complete file does not guarantee approval; the Embassy may request additional documents at its discretion, and holding a pre-enrolment letter does not guarantee the visa will be issued.
4.3 Processing Time: each National (long-stay) visa application is assessed individually; no fixed processing time. Applicants are strongly advised not to confirm flights or other travel arrangements until the visa has actually been granted.
4.4 Forms to Download: Long-Stay (National) Visa Application Form; list of approved insurance companies.

5. FINANCIAL EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS
Under Italian Interior Ministry rules (Directive of 1 March 2000, based on Legislative Decree 286/1998), every traveller must show enough money to cover their stay and return journey. Applies to entry into Italy generally; evidence can be requested again by Border Police even after the visa is issued.
- 1–5 days (fixed amount): €269.60 (one traveller) / €212.81 (two+ travellers per group)
- 6–10 days (per person/day): €44.93 (one) / €26.33 (two+)
- 11–20 days (fixed + daily/person): €51.64 + €36.67/day (one) / €25.82 + €22.21/day (two+)
- Over 20 days (fixed + daily/person): €206.58 + €27.89/day (one) / €118.79 + €17.04/day (two+)
Acceptable evidence: liquid cash; bank guarantees, insurance-policy guarantees or equivalent credit instruments; vouchers for prepaid services; other documents proving access to sources of income in Italy. Failure to demonstrate sufficient means is grounds for refusal, and even a traveller reaching the border without adequate proof can be denied entry by Border Police.

6. TRAVEL & HEALTH INSURANCE
Minimum cover €30,000, valid across the whole Schengen Area (not Italy alone); must cover medical treatment, hospitalisation and repatriation costs; must be valid for the entire duration of the intended stay; policy should come from a company on the VFS/Embassy's approved insurance-provider list.

7. PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS
Sharp focus, clear, good contrast; printed on high-quality photo paper, high resolution; in colour, 35–40 mm wide; no older than 6 months and must resemble the applicant; taken facing the camera directly against a light background; whole head and top of shoulders visible, face occupies 70–80% of the frame; eyes open and clearly visible, hair must not cover the eyes; glasses allowed if eyes clearly visible (no tinted lenses, no frame covering eyes, no glare/reflection); religious headwear allowed provided chin, forehead and both cheeks clearly visible with no shadows across the face. A photo booth meeting these specifications is available on-site at the Visa Application Centre.

8. MINORS TRAVELLING / APPLYING
- Visa application form for a minor (under 18) must be signed by both parents.
- If a minor travels with only one parent: written consent from the other parent plus a photocopy of that parent's CNIC.
- A Birth Certificate (issued by NADRA) is required as proof of the parent-child relationship where applicable, alongside the Family Registration Certificate.
- For the visa to be printed, a minor's passport must be signed (or thumb-impressed) by the minor themself, not a parent, as Pakistani passports are only valid with the holder's own signature or thumb impression.
Start gathering consent letters and NADRA certificates well before your target submission date.

9. MANDATORY POLIO VACCINATION REQUIREMENT
Applies to all applicants from Pakistan. Applicants who are residents of — or have stayed for more than 4 weeks in — Afghanistan, Kenya, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria, Pakistan, Somalia or Syria must hold an international polio vaccination certificate showing vaccination against polio between 4 weeks and 12 months before the travel date. This is an additional eligibility requirement both for the visa to be issued and for crossing the external Schengen border on arrival — keep the original certificate with travel documents, not just a photocopy in the visa file.

10. APPLICATION WORKFLOW (STEP-BY-STEP)
1. Identify your jurisdiction (Karachi vs Islamabad) based on place of residence.
2. Choose the correct visa category — short-stay Schengen (tourist, or study ≤90 days) vs National/Long-Stay (study >90 days).
3. Gather documents early — passport, photos, financial evidence, insurance, employer/university letters, NADRA documents, and the polio vaccination certificate.
4. Book VFS appointment for your jurisdiction, respecting minimum lead times (3–4 weeks Karachi; at least 30 days Islamabad).
5. Pay visa fee and VFS service fee — both in cash, in PKR, at the Visa Application Centre on appointment day.
6. Attend appointment — submit full document set, photo taken at centre if needed, provide biometrics.
7. Track and collect passport using the VFS/consulate tracking service.
