GERMANY — Visa Application Guide for Pakistani Applicants
Source type: Independent informational summary based on the official pages of the German Missions in Pakistan (Embassy Islamabad and Consulate General Karachi, via pakistan.diplo.de).
Covers: Student Visa, and two Short-Stay/Schengen visa categories — Tourist and Visiting Friends/Family. Germany's visa process is almost entirely digital via the Consular Services Portal and the Videx online system.

FRAUD WARNING
Appointments cannot be booked by phone or unofficial agents; only genuine confirmation emails from official portals are valid. Never pay a third party claiming to guarantee an appointment slot.

1. KNOW YOUR VISA TYPE
- Schengen Short-Stay Visa (Cat. C): Tourism/sightseeing, visiting friends/family, business, medical treatment, short training. Max 90 days in any 180-day period across the Schengen Area.
- National (Long-Stay) Visa (Cat. D) — Student: Enrolment in a German university/study programme. Applied for via the Consular Services Portal; leads to a German residence title after arrival.
- Airport Transit Visa (Cat. A): Transit through the international zone of a German airport only; does not permit entry into Germany.

2. ISLAMABAD VS KARACHI — JURISDICTION
- Embassy of Germany, Islamabad: covers Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, and Punjab. Student visa applications go through the Consular Services Portal linked to this mission.
- Consulate General of Germany, Karachi: covers Sindh and Balochistan. The Consular Services Portal for student visas is also active for Karachi applicants via the same online system.
- For short-stay (Schengen) visas: applications via the Videx online system, then submitted at the Visa Section serving your place of residence.

3. VISA FEES (GENERAL)
- Schengen Visa (category "C"): €90.00
- Schengen Visa — children age 6–12: €45.00
- Airport transit visa (category "A"): €90.00
- Long-term/National visa (category "D"): €75.00
- Family reunion — children age 0–17: €37.50
- Dependents of EU citizens: Exempt
Fees payable in PKR in cash at the counter, at the current exchange rate, at both Islamabad Embassy and Karachi Consulate-General.
Standard Schengen processing time: around 14 calendar days from the date the complete application is submitted. National (long-stay) visa processing times vary widely depending on purpose of stay and completeness/quality of documents.
Remonstration procedure abolished: as of 1 July 2025, the voluntary objection ("remonstration") procedure for an initial visa refusal has been permanently cancelled. No informal review route exists if refused — prepare documentation carefully the first time.

4. STUDENT VISA — CONSULAR SERVICES PORTAL PROCEDURE
Fully digital process.
4.1 How it works:
Step 1. Register and complete the interactive questionnaire on the Consular Services Portal (appointment registrations handled entirely through this portal).
Step 2. Required document list generated automatically based on your specific situation.
Step 3. Scan and upload all requested documents through the portal.
Step 4. A visa officer performs initial screening of uploaded documents.
Step 5. Outcome: (1) appointment notification, or (2) request for further/corrected documents.
Incomplete applications directly slow processing; demand exceeds capacity — avoid unnecessary status follow-ups.
4.2 Mandatory documents (read carefully): due to a known technical issue, the portal sometimes marks these as "optional" when they are, in fact, mandatory:
- Past degree certificates (school, Bachelor's, or previous Master's degrees, as applicable)
- Degree transcripts
- Proof of payment of tuition fees, where applicable
Submitting these upfront (even though labeled optional) makes the application run noticeably faster.
4.3 Preparing documents — general advice: prepare all documents/confirmations well in advance; use well-readable A4 copies; sort documents in the correct order specified by the portal. Well-prepared files benefit everyone in the queue.
4.4 Karachi applicants: the Consular Services Portal is also active for Karachi; Sindh/Balochistan residents submit student visa applications through the same online portal as Islamabad-jurisdiction applicants.

5. TOURIST VISA (SCHENGEN SHORT-STAY)
Applications via the Videx online system. Include every listed document in exactly the order described; missing even one document can lead to immediate rejection; processing does not begin until the file is complete. All photocopies in A4 format.
Document checklist:
1. Current & all previous passports, plus visas — originals of all passports; copies of pages 1–2 of the current one; valid ≥3 months beyond visa expiry; must be signed by the holder (unsigned Pakistani passports are invalid).
2. Videx visa application form — signed original, printed after completing Videx online.
3. Two biometric passport photos — original, no older than 6 months; one stapled, one paper-clipped.
4. Health/travel insurance policy — signed original + 1 copy; minimum cover €30,000, valid for all Schengen states (not Germany alone); must be from an approved provider.
5. Proof of accommodation — copy/printout covering the whole stay (hotel bookings + full itinerary).
6. Flight reservation — copy/printout of confirmed round-trip tickets covering the stay.
7. Employment/university confirmation + NOC — original letter stating position/study status, duration, and permission for the trip.
8. Previous visa refusals — copy of any earlier refusal; non-disclosure is treated as fraud and automatic grounds for refusal.
9. CNIC — copy.
10. FRC / Marriage / Birth Certificate — original NADRA certificate(s) as applicable (Birth Certificate for minor applicants).
11. List of children — signed original, official template.
12. List of relatives living abroad — signed original, official template.
13. Financial evidence — bank statements (last 6 months) and salary slips (last 3 months) at minimum; pension books, investment certificates or property documents may supplement, with English translations.
14. Appointment email — printout showing the email address registered with the Embassy.

6. VISITING FAMILY OR FRIENDS (SCHENGEN SHORT-STAY)
Document set overlaps almost entirely with the Tourist visa, with one key difference — proof of invitation from the host:
- Invitation letter & assurances: either (a) a formal letter of obligation (Verpflichtungserklärung) issued by the host's local Foreigners Office (Ausländerbehörde) under §§66–68 of the Residence Act, less than 6 months old, or (b) an informal letter of invitation signed by the host with copies of the host's passport, residence permit and certificate of residence. If not staying with the host, confirmed hotel bookings or other accommodation proof required instead.
- All other documents: same as the Tourist visa checklist (passport(s), Videx form, biometric photos, health insurance min. €30,000 Schengen-wide, flight reservation, employment/university confirmation + NOC, previous refusals, CNIC, FRC/marriage/birth certificate, list of children, list of relatives abroad, financial evidence, appointment email printout).
An incomplete or incorrectly ordered application can be rejected immediately; any undisclosed previous visa refusal is treated as fraud.

7. BLOCKED ACCOUNT & FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS
For long-stay (including student) visa applicants, showing sufficient funds is central. Three recognised routes:
7.1 Blocked bank account (Sperrkonto):
- Current requirement: from 1 January 2025, €992.00 per month must be deposited, i.e. €11,904.00 per year. Applies to all visa applications submitted from that date onward. The Embassy/Consulate-General may require funds for up to two years in particular cases.
- Any blocked-account provider is acceptable provided it offers authorities a login/verification code or direct contact for verification.
- Signature on account-opening documents must be attested by Embassy Islamabad, Consulate-General Karachi, or the Honorary Consul in Lahore.
- Bring passport and a black-and-white copy of the signature page to the attestation appointment.
- Attestation fee: €25 (approx. PKR 5,400, subject to exchange-rate change), payable in exact amount — if exact change unavailable, rebooking is required.
- The attestation appointment and the visa-submission appointment must be booked separately.
7.2 Deed of obligation (Verpflichtungserklärung): a sponsor residing in Germany can sign a declaration of liability under §§66–68 of the Residence Act at their local Migration Office, proving sufficient means (e.g. 6 months of payslips and bank statements). The sponsor takes on responsibility for costs incurred during the stay.
7.3 Official scholarship: a scholarship awarded by a Pakistani university alone is NOT sufficient. Only scholarships awarded by the HEC (Higher Education Commission), or a German/other foreign official scholarship body, are accepted.

8. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS — QUICK REFERENCE
Student Visa: Passport, CNIC; Consular Services Portal questionnaire (online); Degree certificates, transcripts, tuition-fee payment proof, admission letter; Blocked account (€992/month or €11,904/year) OR deed of obligation OR HEC/official scholarship; Health insurance appropriate to stay; Proof of housing arrangements; Family documents as requested case-by-case.
Tourist / Visiting Family: Passport (+ all previous passports), CNIC; Videx form printed & signed; N/A academic docs unless employer/university letter needed; Bank statements (6 months) + salary slips (3 months), supplemented as needed; Min. €30,000 Schengen-wide insurance; Hotel bookings / host's letter of obligation or informal invitation; FRC, marriage/birth certificate, list of children, list of relatives abroad.

9. APPLICATION-DAY CHECKLIST
- Printed appointment confirmation email (showing registered email address)
- Original passport(s) — current and all previous — all signed by the holder
- Completed & signed application form (Videx printout for short-stay; portal-generated set for student visa)
- Two biometric photos, no older than 6 months
- Complete document set in the order specified by the portal/Embassy, copies in A4 format
- Visa fee in cash, in PKR, exact amount if possible
- Blocked-account attestation receipt or deed of obligation (for long-stay/student applicants)

10. FRAUD & APPOINTMENT WARNINGS
- All appointment bookings go through the official Consular Services Portal or Videx system; no third party is authorised to sell or reserve slots; only trust confirmation emails generated directly by official portals.
- Do not disclose Consular Services Portal or Videx login credentials to anyone.
- Verify any communication claiming to be from the Embassy/Consulate against the official domain (diplo.de).
- Report suspected fraud/scam offers to the Embassy/Consulate-General directly.
